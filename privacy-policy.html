Privacy Policy — Mafia: Nightfall

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Tin Compass Studio (“we”, “us”) develops Mafia: Nightfall (the “Game”) for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the Game and the services inside it collect, what passes through our game server when you play online, and what we do and do not keep.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. You never give us an email address, password or real name.
  • Your progress, chips, purchases and settings are stored on your device.
  • Pass-and-play (one phone passed around the table) and the Speakeasy (everyone's phone on the same Wi-Fi) never touch our server.
  • Online play goes through our game server. It sees the display name you type, your moves and your chat messages while the game is running, and keeps none of it once the room closes — except a chat message someone reports, which goes to a person for review.
  • The Game includes third-party services that collect data of their own: Google AdMob (advertising) with Google User Messaging Platform (ad consent), Google Play Billing (purchases on Android) and Apple App Store / StoreKit (purchases on iOS).

Data stored on your device

The Game saves the following locally, using your platform's standard app storage. It never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and uninstalling the Game deletes it:

  • Your chip balance, the card backs, table themes and narrator packs you own, and which ones you have selected
  • Which purchases the Game has applied, so it can grant them (and remove them if a purchase is refunded)
  • Settings: music, sound and narrator volume, narrator on/off and voice, haptics and reduce motion
  • Simple totals: games finished and wins for each side
  • For pass-and-play: the player names and seats of your last table, recently used names, your last game rules, and a game in progress so it can be resumed
  • For playing together: the display name and avatar you chose, and a random seat ticket so you can rejoin a room you were in
  • Counters used to pace ads and rewards (games since the last ad, rewarded ads watched today, the daily bonus date)
  • Whether you have already seen How to play

The narrator speaks using your device's built-in text-to-speech; the lines are generated in the Game, on your device.

Playing together

The Speakeasy (same Wi-Fi)

One phone hosts the room and the others connect to it directly over your local network. Nothing goes to our server or the internet. On iOS the Game asks for permission to find devices on your local network; it uses it only for this. Traffic between phones on the same Wi-Fi is not encrypted, so play on a network you trust.

Online rooms and Quick Play

Online games run on a game server we operate, hosted by Render in Singapore. Connections to it are encrypted. While you play online, the server handles:

  • The display name and avatar you choose (it does not need to be your real name — please don't use it if you'd rather not)
  • Your game actions (night choices, votes) and the chat messages you send
  • A random seat token, so your phone can rejoin its seat after a dropped connection
  • Your IP address, which any internet connection needs. Our hosting provider may keep it in short-lived operational logs

Rooms are held only in the server's memory. They are not written to a database, and when a room closes or everyone leaves, its names, moves and chat are gone. Other players in your room see your display name, avatar and chat messages; in Quick Play these are strangers.

The Game contains no voice chat and never uses your microphone or camera.

Chat safety and reports

Chat messages are filtered for slurs and profanity before anyone sees them, and any player can mute another. If you report a message, the following is sent to a private moderation channel (on Discord) that only we can read: the reported message, the display names of the person who sent it and of the person who reported it, the room code and the chat channel. We use this only to review abuse and improve the filters, and delete it when it is no longer needed for that. A player reported by two different people is muted for the rest of that room automatically.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game shows two kinds of ad: an occasional full-screen ad between games (never shown if you own Remove Ads or the Supporter Bundle, and never in the middle of a game), and optional “watch a video for chips” rewarded ads that only play when you choose them.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data directly — we never see it. Depending on your settings and region, AdMob may collect:

  • Your advertising identifier — the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking when Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, coarse region)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed, clicked)

This is used to deliver and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalise them. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Consent and tracking choices

  • Ad consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the Game shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. Where you are entitled to revisit that choice, the Game's Settings → Privacy options reopens it at any time.
  • Tracking on iOS. The Game asks once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier. Choosing “Ask App Not to Track” still plays the whole Game; ads are simply less relevant. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases: chip packs (consumable), and the one-time Remove Ads and Supporter Bundle. There are no subscriptions, and nothing you can buy changes the rules or gives anyone an advantage in a game. Payments are processed by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iOS.

We never receive or store your payment details. We do not see your card number, billing address, name or account details. The Game receives only a confirmation from the store that a specific product was purchased, which it uses to grant the item. Purchase history and payment data are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Refunds are decided by Google Play or Apple under their own policies. If a one-time purchase is refunded, the Game removes what it granted.

What we do not collect

We run no account system. We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera input, or files. The Game contains no analytics SDK and no crash reporting.

Children's privacy

Mafia: Nightfall is a social deduction party game with a fictional crime theme (players are “eliminated” at night and by vote, with no graphic content). It is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Quick Play lets players chat with strangers; chat is filtered and can be muted and reported, but we ask that a parent or guardian supervise younger players and their purchases. Google Play's purchase authentication and Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time offer controls for this.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information in the Game, contact us and we will delete any report that contains it.

Your rights

We hold no account or profile about you. Online rooms leave nothing behind on our server; the only thing we may keep is a reported chat message.
